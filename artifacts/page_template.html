Learning to get further right

Finding

A stall that appeared mid-training, then resolved

At around 500K steps, asking the agent for its single best action at every step produced a striking failure: it sprinted to x ≈ 2370, then stood perfectly still until the in-game clock ran out — about 90% of the episode motionless. Sampling from that same policy cleared the level.

By 1M steps the behaviour was gone: every run's greedy policy now finishes 1-1. The stall was a transient artifact of a half-trained policy rather than a property of the converged agent — a narrow failure of action selection, not of what was learned. It is consistent with yumouwei's implementation, where a single-frame agent gets stuck and temporal context is what lets it commit to jumps. Clear rates here are reported from the sampled policy throughout.
